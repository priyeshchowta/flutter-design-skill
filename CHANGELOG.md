# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-09)

First public release.

- Router skill with a Design Read, three dials, and ten commands.
- 111 rules with Incorrect / Correct Dart, impact, and severity.
- 30 palettes, 20 font pairings, 24 styles, 14 motion presets. `search.dart --design-system` prints a `ColorScheme` and `TextTheme` starter.
- `audit.dart` flags slop, missing semantics, and off-grid literals.
- Golden-matrix template and a habit-tracker before / after example.
- Install notes for Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Codex, Gemini CLI, and Windsurf.
